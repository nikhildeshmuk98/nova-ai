What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit NOVA AI. These files help the platform recognize your browser, remember preferences, improve performance, and provide secure personalized experiences.

⚡

Fast Experience

Cookies help pages load faster and remember your AI settings instantly.

🔐

Secure Sessions

They help keep your account secure and maintain safe login sessions.

🎯

Personalization

Your theme, language, and preferences stay synced across the platform.

🎨
Theme Preferences

📊
Analytics

Understand platform performance and improve future updates.
